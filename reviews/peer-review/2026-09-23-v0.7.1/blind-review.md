# Blind review PasskeyTransit v0.7

Provisional verdict: **Borderline**. Confidence: **3/5**.

The manuscript presents a clear measurement harness for semantic preservation in
passkey exchange and carefully limits its conclusions to synthetic controls. Its
main strength is the separation of successful authentication from extension,
identity, path, and operational outcomes. Its main weakness, on the manuscript
alone, is that the reported percentages are generated by deliberately selected
reference policies and therefore do not estimate provider behavior or prevalence.

The prose discloses that limitation, treats the 96 route-by-stratum cells as the
principal descriptive result, calls aggregate percentages design-weighted, and
classifies C3 as an in-memory simulation. Acceptance therefore depends on whether
the venue values a reproducible method and artifact without real-provider
validation. The manuscript alone cannot substantiate its reproducibility claims,
the retained signatures, or the raw-to-publication lineage.
